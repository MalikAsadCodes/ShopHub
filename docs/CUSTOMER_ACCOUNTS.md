# Sample customer accounts

When the owner ticks **"Add sample products, orders and 50 customer accounts"** on first run,
ShopHub creates these 50 customer accounts. Sign in with any of them to try the shop.

- **Password rule:** first name + `123` (for example `Ayesha123`, `Muhammad123`).
- Emails use `example.com`, a domain reserved for examples, so none of them belongs to a real person.
- These are sample accounts with simple, public passwords. **Don't load sample data in a real shop.**
- The same list is in [`customer_accounts.csv`](customer_accounts.csv) (opens in Excel).
  A test checks that this list and the app's sample data always match.

(Rs 87,899 in total when the sample data is loaded); Aimal's khata is open but unused.
Sign in as one of them and choose **Khata (pay later)** at checkout, or open **My khata**.
